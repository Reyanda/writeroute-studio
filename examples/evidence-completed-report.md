Approve a monthly stock-out verification rule assigning district pharmacy managers to verify facility reports by the fifth working day of each month. The fixed deadline would allow the district team to identify missing submissions and correct stock records before the monthly review.

The assessment covered 17 facility reports. 11 facilities reported at least one stock-out between April and June 2026. The median duration was 6 days, with a range of 1 to 19 days.
